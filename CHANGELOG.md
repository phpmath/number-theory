## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- First release.
- Divisibility and extended gcd, modular arithmetic, the Chinese remainder theorem, primality, a prime sieve, factorization, totient, symbols and sequences, on top of `phpmath/biginteger`.
- A documentation site at https://phpmath.github.io/number-theory/.
